Blockcade Privacy Policy

Pro Paragon Software LLC · Effective 30-SEP-2026

The short version
  • We don't ask for your name, email or any account of our own, and we don't run servers that store information about you.
  • Your progress is saved on your device and, if you sign in, in your own Google Play Games account.
  • Blockcade shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may use your device's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure those ads, and where the law requires it we ask for your consent first.
  • Blockcade is not directed at children under 13.

1. Who we are

Blockcade is a puzzle game made by Pro Paragon Software LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Blockcade app on Android and, when released, iOS.

Contact: support@proparagonsoftware.com

3. Information stored on your device

Blockcade saves your level progress, best times and scores, the items you have, your recent level attempts, and your sound, music and haptics settings on your device. It also keeps a few counters that pace ads, such as when an ad was last shown. This information stays on your device unless you sign in to Google Play Games (see below). Uninstalling the game removes it.

4. Google Play Games (sign-in and cloud save)

On Android, Blockcade can sign you in to Google Play Games Services so your progress follows you between devices. When you are signed in, Google provides us with your Play Games player ID, and your saved game (the progress, items and settings described above) is stored in the Saved Games area of your own Google account. We do not receive your name, email address or contacts.

Google's handling of this information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can review or delete Play Games data from your Google account and the Google Play Games settings on your device.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Blockcade is free and supported by ads, provided by Google AdMob. There are two kinds:

  • Occasional full-screen ads between levels.
  • Optional rewarded ads you can choose to watch to get more of an item you have run out of.

To serve these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google your device's advertising ID, information about your device and the app (such as model, operating system and language), your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), and information about your interaction with ads (such as views and taps). Google and its advertising partners use this to show ads, cap how often you see them, measure their performance, and detect fraud and abuse. Where you have consented, or where consent is not required, ads may be personalized based on your interests.

Learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services and about Google's advertising.

We set the ads in Blockcade to a maximum content rating suitable for a general audience, and we block several sensitive ad categories.

6. Your choices and consent

European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland

If you are in these regions, Blockcade asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before ads can be personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad Privacy → Edit. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

United States

Depending on your state, you may have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. Where this applies, Google's consent tool presents that choice in the game.

Everyone

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your device's settings (on Android, usually Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads; on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Don't sign in to Google Play Games if you prefer your progress to stay only on your device.
  • Uninstall the game to remove the information stored on your device.

7. Children

Blockcade is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. What we don't collect

Blockcade does not ask you to create an account with us, and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. It does not currently offer in-app purchases. If we add purchases in the future, payments will be handled by Google Play or the App Store, and we will update this policy.

9. Keeping and deleting information

Information on your device is kept until you uninstall the game. Your cloud save is kept in your Google account until you delete it through Google. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies. Because we do not run servers that store information about you, we generally have no copy to delete; if you have a request, contact us and we will help you with it or direct you to Google.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or limit the use of your personal information, and to object to or withdraw consent for certain processing. To exercise them, contact us at support@proparagonsoftware.com. You can also exercise many of these rights directly with Google through your Google account.

11. Security and international transfers

Information stored with Google is protected by Google's security measures. Google may process information in the United States and other countries where it or its partners operate, under the safeguards described in Google's policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We will update this page if the game's handling of information changes, and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's store listing or release notes.
